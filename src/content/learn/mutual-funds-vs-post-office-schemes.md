**Post office savings schemes** (PPF, NSC, KVP, SCSS, MIS, RD and time deposits) pay a rate set every quarter by the Ministry of Finance and are backed by the Government of India. **Mutual funds** pool money into shares, bonds or both, and their value moves with markets. The choice depends on the goal, not on which is "better" in general.

## Current post office rates (October–December 2026)

| Scheme | Rate a year | Term |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PPF | 7.1% | 15 years |
| Sukanya Samriddhi | 8.2% | 21 years |
| Senior Citizens' Savings Scheme | 8.2% | 5 years |
| NSC | 7.7% | 5 years |
| KVP | 7.5% (doubles in 115 months) | 115 months |
| Monthly Income Scheme | 7.4% | 5 years |
| RD | 6.7% | 5 years |
| Time deposit, 5 years | 7.5% | 5 years |

Each scheme has a calculator: start from the [government schemes page](/schemes/).

## How they compare

| | Post office schemes | Mutual funds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Return | Fixed at the rate on the day you invest | Market-linked, not guaranteed |
| Safety | Government-backed | Depends on the fund; can fall |
| Lock-in | Fixed terms; early exit restricted | Most can be redeemed any day (ELSS: 3 years) |
| Tax | PPF and SSY tax-free; others mostly taxed at slab rate | Equity gains taxed lightly when sold; debt gains at slab rate |
| Tax deduction | PPF, SSY, NSC, SCSS and 5-year TD under section 80C (old regime) | Only ELSS under 80C |

## An example

₹1,00,000 in NSC at 7.7% grows to about ₹1,44,903 in 5 years, a certain amount, with the interest taxable. The same ₹1,00,000 in an equity fund could be worth much more or less after 5 years: across large cap funds, 5-year returns have ranged widely, and a 5-year period that started near a market peak has sometimes ended with little gain.

## A way to decide

1. **Money you cannot afford to see fall**, or need on a fixed date within 5 years: post office schemes or bank deposits.
2. **Long goals of 10 or more years**: equity funds have the better long-run record; PPF and SSY suit the safe part of the same goal.
3. **Tax**: in the old tax regime, PPF and SSY give a deduction and tax-free returns, which is hard for any taxable product to beat after tax. See [PPF vs mutual funds](/learn/ppf-vs-mutual-funds/).
4. **Regular income**: SCSS and MIS pay a fixed amount; a fund SWP can last longer but is not guaranteed.
