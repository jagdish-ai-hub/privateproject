Equity funds are grouped partly by the size of the companies they own. SEBI ranks every listed company by its full market value: the **top 100 are large caps**, **101 to 250 are mid caps**, and **251 onwards are small caps**. A large cap fund must keep at least 80% in large caps; mid cap and small cap funds at least 65% in their own segment. See all [categories](/learn/mutual-fund-categories-india/).

## How they have behaved

Median Direct-plan fund in each category, three years to early November 2026:

| | Large cap | Mid cap | Small cap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Return a year, 3 years | about 12.2% | about 16.4% | about 13.5% |
| Return a year, 5 years | about 9.5% | about 14.7% | about 14.6% |
| Volatility a year | about 13.5% | about 16.5% | about 16.9% |
| Worst fall from a peak | about 16% | about 21% | about 24% |

These are past figures for one window and they change every day. The live numbers are on the [large cap](/category/large-cap/), [mid cap](/category/mid-cap/) and [small cap](/category/small-cap/) pages. Over other periods the order of returns has flipped: there have been long stretches when small caps trailed large caps badly.

## Why smaller means bumpier

Smaller companies can grow faster, but they have fewer customers, thinner finances and fewer buyers for their shares. In a market fall, investors sell them first and buy them back last, so the falls are deeper and the recoveries take longer. A small cap fund also has fewer shares to choose from, so a very large small cap fund can find it harder to buy and sell.

## Matching to time horizon

- **Large cap**: 5 years or more; the steadiest of the three, and the easiest place to use a low-cost [index fund](/learn/index-funds-vs-etfs-vs-active-funds/).
- **Mid cap**: 7 years or more, and you can live with a 25% to 30% fall.
- **Small cap**: 7 to 10 years or more, as a smaller part of your equity money, and only if a 40% fall would not make you sell.

Flexi cap and large and mid cap funds mix sizes, so the fund manager decides the balance.

## How to compare funds within a size

Compare a small cap fund only with other small cap funds. Look at 3-year and 5-year returns, the worst fall and [risk measures](/learn/mutual-fund-risk-measures/) such as volatility, the expense ratio and the fund size. You can line up to four funds on one chart with [side by side](/compare/).
