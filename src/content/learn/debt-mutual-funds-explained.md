A **debt fund** lends money, mostly by buying bonds and money-market instruments issued by governments, banks, public sector companies and corporations. Returns come from interest and from price changes in those bonds.

## The risks

1. **Interest-rate risk.** When interest rates rise, the price of existing bonds falls, and so does a bond fund's NAV. The longer a fund's bonds last (its **duration**), the bigger the effect.
2. **Credit risk.** The borrower may delay or fail to repay. Funds holding lower-rated bonds earn more interest to compensate for this risk.
3. **Liquidity risk.** In stress, some bonds are hard to sell quickly, which can force a fund to sell at a discount or limit redemptions.

Debt funds are not fixed deposits. Their returns are not guaranteed, and the value can fall.

## Common categories

| Category | What it does | Typical risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overnight | Lends for 1 day at a time | Lowest |
| Liquid | Instruments up to 91 days | Very low |
| Ultra short / low duration | Roughly 3 to 12 months | Low |
| Money market | Instruments up to 1 year | Low |
| Short / medium duration | Roughly 1 to 4 years | Moderate |
| Long duration / gilt | Many years, mostly government bonds | Higher interest-rate risk |
| Corporate bond | Mostly high-rated company bonds | Moderate |
| Credit risk | Lower-rated bonds for higher yield | Highest credit risk |

Match the fund's duration to when you need the money. A fund whose bonds last far longer than your holding period can lose value in the meantime.

## How to compare them

- Look at **returns over several periods**, not just the latest one. Short-term debt fund returns move with interest rates.
- Check the **volatility and maximum drawdown** on each [fund page](/); in debt funds, a large drawdown is a warning sign of credit or duration risk.
- Compare **within the category** only.
- Read the **riskometer** and the portfolio in the fund's monthly disclosure for credit quality.

## Tax

Gains on most debt funds bought after 1 April 2023 are added to your income and taxed at your slab rate. See [mutual fund taxation](/learn/mutual-fund-taxation-india/).

## Liquid and overnight funds on this site

Their daily NAV changes are tiny, so one-year returns look like a fixed deposit rate. Some of these funds have also changed the face value of their units, which would look like a large fall in NAV. The site detects and adjusts for such changes so returns are not distorted.
