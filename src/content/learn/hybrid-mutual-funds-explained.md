A **hybrid mutual fund** holds more than one kind of asset in one fund, usually [equity](/learn/mutual-fund-categories-india/) and debt. The mix sets how much the fund can rise and how far it can fall. SEBI defines seven hybrid categories.

## The seven types

| Category | Equity share | Behaves most like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conservative hybrid | 10% to 25% | A debt fund with a little equity |
| Balanced hybrid | 40% to 60% | Half and half |
| Aggressive hybrid | 65% to 80% | An equity fund with some cushion |
| Balanced advantage (dynamic asset allocation) | 0% to 100%, changed by the fund's own model | Varies with market levels |
| Multi asset allocation | At least 10% each in three asset classes, such as equity, debt and gold | A spread across assets |
| Arbitrage | Equity bought and sold at the same time to lock a small price gap | A low-risk short-term fund |
| Equity savings | Equity, part of it hedged, plus at least 10% debt | Between debt and aggressive hybrid |

## How much they fell

Over the three years to early November 2026, the median Direct-plan fund's worst fall from a peak was about 13% for aggressive hybrid funds, 9% for balanced advantage funds, 3.5% for conservative hybrid funds and less than 1% for arbitrage funds. A typical large cap fund fell about 16% over the same period. Lower falls have usually come with lower returns in strong markets. The [risk measures guide](/learn/mutual-fund-risk-measures/) explains worst fall and volatility.

## Who each type suits

- **Arbitrage**: money parked for a few months by investors who want equity-fund taxation and very small swings.
- **Conservative hybrid and equity savings**: savers who want a little more than debt funds and can accept small dips.
- **Balanced advantage and multi asset**: first-time equity investors who want the fund to manage the equity share.
- **Aggressive hybrid**: long-term investors who want mostly equity with a debt cushion.

## Tax depends on the equity share

Funds that keep at least 65% in Indian equity are taxed like equity funds. Funds that hold mostly debt are taxed at your slab rate. Funds in between have their own rule. Because the label alone does not settle this, check the fund's scheme documents, and see [mutual fund taxation](/learn/mutual-fund-taxation-india/).

## How to compare hybrid funds

Compare funds only within the same hybrid category, since a conservative hybrid fund and an aggressive hybrid fund are built for different jobs. Look at 3-year and 5-year returns, the worst fall, how often 1-year returns were positive ([rolling returns](/learn/rolling-returns-explained/)), and the expense ratio.
