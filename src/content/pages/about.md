Mutual Fund Compare is a free tool for comparing mutual funds in India. It takes the official daily NAV of every scheme and calculates returns, SIP returns and risk measures so that you can filter and compare funds without signing up for anything.

## What it does

- Lists every active scheme and lets you filter by category, plan, option, returns, volatility and more.
- Shows each fund's NAV history, returns over several periods, SIP returns, risk and rolling returns.
- Compares up to four funds on one chart.
- Offers simple calculators and plain-language [guides](/learn/).

## What it does not do

It does not recommend funds, rate them, or tell you what to buy. Rankings are orderings of data, such as "sorted by 3-year return", not advice. It is not registered as an investment adviser or research analyst. See the [disclaimer](/disclaimer/).

## Where the data comes from

The data comes from AMFI, the industry body that publishes the NAV of every scheme, delivered through [MFapi.in](https://www.mfapi.in). Every calculation is explained on the [methodology](/methodology/) page, including how stock splits and bad data points are handled.

## Independence and funding

The site is independent. It is not affiliated with AMFI, MFapi.in, SEBI or any fund house, and no fund house pays to appear or to be placed higher. The site is free to use and may in future show advertising to cover its costs; if that happens it will be clearly marked and will never change the data or the order of funds.
