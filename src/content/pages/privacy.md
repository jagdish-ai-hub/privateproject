**Summary:** there is nothing to sign up for, and the site does not ask for or store your name, email, phone number or financial details.

## What is stored in your browser

The site saves a few preferences on your own device using local storage: your light or dark theme, which table columns you chose and the table density. They never leave your browser. You can clear them in your browser settings at any time.

## Your filters and selections

The filters, sort order and funds you choose are kept in the page address (for example `?cat=Small%20Cap`) so that you can share or bookmark them. The address is processed in your browser; the fund list itself is a static file and the site does not receive your filter choices.

## Server logs

Like any website, the hosting provider receives your IP address, browser type and the pages requested when you load a page, and may keep these in standard logs for security and operation. We do not use these logs to identify you.

## Analytics and cookies

The site uses Microsoft Clarity, a free analytics service from Microsoft, to understand how pages are used: where visitors click and scroll, which pages they open and where they get stuck. Clarity sets cookies and records anonymised interaction data (clicks, scrolling, mouse movement, device and browser type, approximate location and the pages visited). It masks what you type into form fields, and we do not send it your name, email or financial details because the site never asks for them. The data is processed by Microsoft under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement). The site does not currently use advertising cookies. If that changes, this page will be updated first and a consent notice will be shown where the law requires it.

## Advertising

The site does not currently show advertising. If it does in future, advertising partners such as Google may use cookies to show and measure ads, and you will be able to control this through the consent notice and through Google's ad settings. This page will describe the change before ads appear.

## Third parties

Fund data is fetched from public sources (AMFI and MFapi.in) when the site's data is prepared. Your browser does not contact these services when you use the site. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. The one exception is Microsoft Clarity analytics, described above, which your browser contacts while you use the site.

## Your rights and contact

Because the site does not collect personal data, there is usually nothing to access or delete. If you have a privacy question, use the [contact page](/contact/).

## Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top of the page shows when it was last reviewed.
